-- The catalog of prizes, of size N
-- A sequence of M-N public keys
-- A bloom filter, B, loaded with all M-N public keys

Offline:
1)  For each prize k < N
2)  Generate dA, dB, and a unique i < M
3)  Derive PA = dA*G and PB = dB*G
4)  Confirm PA and PB can be inserted to the bloom filter, B
5)  Derive S = dA*PB or dB*PA
6)  Record (k, PA), publicly
7)  Record (dA, dB, i, PB, S), privately
8)  Place PB in the Merkle Tree sequence at i
